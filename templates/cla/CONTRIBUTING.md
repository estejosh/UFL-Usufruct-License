# Contributing

This project is licensed under the Usufruct License (UFL). Read Section 4
of [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE) before you submit anything: a fix, change, or
addition you submit is licensed to the project for good, and you agree to
that by submitting it.

To contribute:

1. Open a pull request. Patches sent any other way are not considered.
2. The CLA check will comment on your pull request. Post the sign comment
   it shows (see [`CLA.md`](./CLA.md)). You sign once; it covers your later
   pull requests.
3. Sign your commits (`git config commit.gpgsign true`, with a GPG, SSH, or
   S/MIME key added to your GitHub account). Unsigned commits are not merged.
4. If an employer or client has rights in your work, get their authority
   before you submit it (Section 4).

A pull request opened by an automated agent is submitted by whoever runs
that agent, and that person or organization signs.
